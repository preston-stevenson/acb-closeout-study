How "beaten" is measured

Cushion
Ball handler's distance to the rim minus his defender's. Positive means the defender is between his man and the basket.
Beaten
Cushion falls to −1 ft or worse and stays there for a fifth of a second, with both players seen on camera.
Beat moment
The start of the attack that ends in the crossing: the ball handler is moving toward the rim at 3+ ft/s and faster than his defender. Each play gets its own moment instead of a fixed lookback.
Closing speed
How fast the defender is moving toward the ball handler, in feet per second.
